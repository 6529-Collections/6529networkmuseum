# Magnum Photos 75

## An archive made visible as a networked edition

Magnum Photos 75 gave historical photographs a new public afterlife through
curatorial selection, serial tokens, metadata, and network publication. The
photographs predate the blockchain by decades. Their 2022 edition placed each
frame beside a collection number, curation field, token, metadata object, image
pointer, edition language, and transaction history.[^1]

The artistic importance of the project lies in that change of encounter. A
viewer approaches an archive image through a numbered edition while older
histories of negative, print, caption, book, and copyright remain active. The
project makes circulation part of the image's history.

## Project and curatorial history

The official launch page announced a first collection in Magnum's seventy-fifth
anniversary year. It describes 76 images, six drops from 28 June to 12 July
2022, and selection by photographer and Obscura co-founder Alejandro Cartagena.
The same publication also uses 75-image wording, so the first-release count is
preserved as an observation boundary ([S04](../sources/source-register.md)).[^2]

The direct metadata describes a three-curation, 225-image project
([S10–S14](../sources/source-register.md); [machine object schedule](../machine/object-schedule.json)). The second
selection is presented in dialogue with Azu Nwagbogu, founder and director of
the African Artists' Foundation. Nwagbogu's account turns from the familiar
“decisive moment” toward an “indecisive moment”: images charged with
anticipation, feeling, intent, and multiple possible readings.[^3]

The launch material presents the first selection through the endurance of
historical photographs. The Museum reads that stated rationale as a concern
with **formal and narrative endurance**: a frame survives because its visual
structure and the story it carries continue to invite attention. That phrase is
an interpretation of the launch's description, not a new title assigned to
Cartagena's selection.[^4]

The selected offer contains one first-register token, Saman #44, and four
second-register tokens: Seymour #127, Towell #145, Bar-Am #97, and Meloni #104
([S10–S14](../sources/source-register.md); [machine object schedule](../machine/object-schedule.json)).
That distribution belongs to the token records. The donor's five-Work relation
is a later formation across the registers.

## Publication and token infrastructure

Each target token links to JSON metadata and an Arweave JPEG through one
reviewed Ethereum ERC-721 contract. Token number, sequence, curation,
archive reference, metadata pointer, image pointer, and transfer history are
publication facts. “1 of 1” describes the token edition. The underlying
photographic Work continues to have other possible manifestations in estates,
prints, books, exhibitions, and agency archives.

The Arweave pointer identifies an upstream source-image URL and observed fixity.
The historical CloudFront URLs are Wave-upload presentation manifestations in
the selected offer's proposal context. The issuer's `All Rights Reserved`
notice remains a rights statement; it is not a general permission for download,
reproduction, or Collection publication ([S10–S14](../sources/source-register.md);
[S38](../sources/source-register.md); [wave-media join](../machine/wave-media-join.json)).

## Relation to the selected offer

Magnum Photos 75 makes selection legible in a form built for network
circulation. The selected *Conflict at Its Edges* offer joins one first-register
token to four second-register tokens, then asks how captions, access histories,
and archive variants behave when a donor forms a new five-Work relation. The
[Curated Acquisition page](conflict-at-its-edges.md) carries the concise case;
the [comparative essay](../essays/conflict-at-its-edges.md) carries the
cross-object interpretation.

## Presentation and rights

The exact historical public Wave URLs may be referenced in the selected-offer
proposal context with their supplied artist/Magnum credits, `All Rights
Reserved`, and **Wave-source historical proposal media** label. The [Source and
rights record](../dossiers/source-and-rights-record.md) carries the current
boundary. Collection publication, download, derivatives, IIIF, and preservation
require later component-specific permission and review ([S38](../sources/source-register.md);
[wave-media join](../machine/wave-media-join.json); [integration map](../machine/integration-map.json)).
