## Rights posture

The source metadata and local proposal records identify photographer/Magnum
copyright notices and record token transfer as asserting no copyright or
reproduction grant. A URL and checksum identify an observed resource; they do
not transfer copyright, establish Museum custody, or authorize a new
manifestation. Current rights terms, transaction-specific permissions, and
component-level display/preservation authority remain research questions.

The proposal media record supports reference or embed in the proposal’s Wave
context. It does not support a download, full-resolution delivery claim, new
crop or derivative, responsive recompression, IIIF service, preservation master,
or permanent Collection publication. Those later uses require written,
component-specific permission and a reviewed media/preservation record.
