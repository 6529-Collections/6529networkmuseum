## Rights posture

The source metadata and local proposal records identify photographer/Magnum
copyright notices and record token transfer without a copyright or reproduction
grant. A URL, checksum, or upstream persistence locator identifies an observed
resource; it does not transfer copyright, establish Museum custody, or authorize
a new manifestation ([S10–S16](../sources/source-register.md); [object schedule](../machine/object-schedule.json)). Current rights terms, transaction-specific permissions,
and component-level display/preservation authority remain research questions
([S09](../sources/source-register.md); [integration map](../machine/integration-map.json)).

The historical public presentation supports reference or embed only after the
selected Work, Media Reference, Curated Acquisition, proposal, and final Wave
publication observation are bound in the same graph relation. A standalone Work
route fails closed until that relation is verified and visibly labelled as
historical Wave proposal presentation. Download, full-resolution delivery, new
crop or derivative, responsive recompression, IIIF service, preservation master,
and permanent Collection publication require written, component-specific
permission and a reviewed media/preservation record ([S38](../sources/source-register.md);
[wave-media join](../machine/wave-media-join.json); [integration map](../machine/integration-map.json)).
