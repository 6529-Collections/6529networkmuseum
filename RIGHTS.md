# Rights map

The 6529 Network Museum publishes a mixed record. Museum-authored writing,
structured data, software, third-party evidence, and artworks do not all carry
the same rights. This file states the repository-wide default and its limits.

## Museum-authored public record

Except where a file or record states different terms, the Museum dedicates its
original public documentation, curatorial writing, metadata, schemas, scripts,
and tests in this repository to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

This implements the Museum's founding CC0 default. It does not change an
explicit rights statement attached to an individual work, accession, source,
or program.

## Artworks, media, and third-party sources

The repository-wide default does not apply to material the Museum did not
create. Artworks, token media, artist texts, platform pages, archival captures,
quoted material, and other third-party evidence remain subject to their own
licenses, copyright, terms, and evidentiary uses. A file's presence in this
repository is not a transfer of copyright and does not imply permission for
uses beyond its stated rights.

Object ownership, token custody, copyright, display permission, preservation
permission, and reuse rights are separate facts. Consult the relevant record
and source before reusing material.

## Contributions

By submitting a contribution, you confirm that you have the right to provide
it. An accepted contribution to Museum-authored material is published under
the default above unless its destination record explicitly states different
terms. Material supplied only as third-party evidence retains its stated
rights and must be clearly identified as such.

Every accepted contribution remains attributable through its commit and pull
request. Material changes to published scholarship or evidence should also
name the contributor or cite the pull request in the relevant record.

## No warranties

Material is provided as-is. The Museum's factual, provenance, rights, and
technical records document the evidence available to it; they are not a
substitute for independent legal or conservation advice.
