## Observation and verification boundary

The live generator was viewed at a 1280 × 720 viewport on 5 November 2026. Successive screenshots following click interaction showed different colours inside a visually stable grey outline, with a coloured round endpoint. The observed samples included green/blue/violet and magenta/red/orange. This supports a qualitative account of local colour change and stable silhouette. It does not establish a timed cycle, all interaction controls, or the initial autoplay state.

Browser version and exact observation timestamps were not recorded. Screenshot bytes were not retained in this package. No image pixel census, source-byte hash, type census, or complete condition report is claimed.

Platform documentation describes reconstructing a Squiggle using on-chain script, token data and the p5.js dependency. This redraft has not independently completed that end-to-end reconstruction for #6529. Its discussion of future care is interpretation and a proposed curatorial responsibility, not certification that preservation is complete.
