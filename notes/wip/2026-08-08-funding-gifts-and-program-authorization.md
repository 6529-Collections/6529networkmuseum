## Working policy distinction

1. **Gifts of art or NFTs:** require approval of the exact gift. Collection preapproval signals collecting interest but does not accept an exact token or group.
2. **Authorized acquisition programs:** should not receive a second curatorial vote when the prior TDH authorization already fixes the premise, budget, selection mechanism, and accession conditions. Later identity, title, rights, technical, payment, and custody checks execute the decision rather than reopening aesthetic selection.
3. **Unrestricted funding:** may be eligible for administrative acceptance when denominated in an expressly approved, narrow set of settlement assets and carrying no donor direction or other restriction.
4. **Restricted funding:** requires approval before receipt because the restriction creates a Museum commitment, even when the contributed asset is ETH or a major stablecoin.
5. **Other fungible assets:** the Museum should avoid discretionary trading and price-timing responsibility. The safest default is donor conversion before donation. Any exceptional Museum-side conversion would require a mechanical, predeclared process, no timing or price promise, and no discretionary portfolio mandate.
6. **Unsolicited transfers:** do not establish acceptance, donor recognition, retention duties, accession, or an obligation to realize a favorable sale price.

## Drafting issue in the active gift proposal

The active proposal *Gift-Specific TDH Approval for Every Museum Gift*, serial `1281404`, uses the phrase “every proposed gift.” Read literally, this may include unrestricted ETH or other funding assets even though the proposal's substance and examples concern gifts of art and collection assets.

A future amendment or interpretive resolution should decide whether the rule covers:

- every proposed gift of art or a collection asset;
- every restricted funding gift;
- unrestricted gifts of approved settlement assets;
- non-approved fungible assets;
- proceeds directed through Meme Card collaborator mechanics; and
- unsolicited transfers.

## Questions requiring an express decision

- Which settlement assets, if any, qualify for unrestricted administrative acceptance?
- Are USDC and USDT treated identically despite issuer, freeze, and custody differences?
- What constitutes a restriction: named artist, named work, medium, time period, program, spending deadline, return right, or donor approval?
- Which prior vote is sufficient to authorize an acquisition program, and what minimum terms must that vote establish?
- May the Museum ever accept a non-approved token for immediate conversion, or must the donor convert it first?
- If Museum-side conversion is allowed, who executes it, under what routing and slippage controls, and with what public disclaimer and record?
- How should a Main Stage Meme Card vote, the Museum Wave, and program-specific selection votes relate without duplicating or contradicting authority?
