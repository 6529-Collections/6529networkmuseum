# Proposed gifts: Magnum Photos 75

Status: active WIP analysis and implementation ledger

## Mandate

Build the Museum's first public proposed-gift dossier around an offer by
punk6529 of five `Magnum Photos 75` ERC-721 works. The public dossier is the
substantive case placed before the Museum Wave. The Wave remains the curatorial
decision-maker: clearing the applicable TDH threshold selects the scheduled
gift for accession processing; it does not itself transfer title, establish
custody, complete acceptance, or assign an accession number.

The release contains no private or restricted diligence layer. Every governed
fact and source used in the Wave dossier is public.

## Public experience decision

The proposal will not become a standing Museum website section or navigation
item. Open gifts belong in the Museum Wave, where the images, argument,
countercase, discussion, TDH votes, and outcome can remain together.

The public presentation is one seven-part Storm drop:

1. exact resolution and five-work schedule;
2. one image-led part for each work;
3. a final synthesis containing the case, countercase, rights and technical
   state, repeated resolution, and governed source edition.

The parent Storm drop is the single voting object. If it does not clear the
threshold, its status becomes **closed without selection** inside the Wave's
historical context. It is not reproduced as a rejection entry beside artists
or collection holdings. If it is selected, the proposal becomes an intake
source for the later accession package and public accession scholarship.

Full artist, project, and object scholarship is deferred until accession. The
Wave dossier must nevertheless be accurate, visually useful, and sufficient
for an informed vote.

## Policy boundary

The adopted donation policy currently requires Wave authorization for this
non-preapproved collection. A separate policy amendment is needed before the
Museum may state as adopted that every exact gift, including gifts from
preapproved collections, requires a Wave accession proposal. This release will
not silently rewrite the adopted policy or combine that institutional-policy
question with the Magnum decision.

## Editorial decisions

- No institutional recommendation or numerical score. The dossier presents
  the strongest case for and against; TDH voters decide.
- The descriptive title is `Five photographs from Magnum Photos 75`; the
  interpretive subtitle is `Authority and uncertainty, 1952–2016`.
- Source captions, visible description, historical context, and interpretation
  remain distinguishable.
- Public Arweave media remains upstream and is not represented as a retained
  preservation object.
- `All Rights Reserved` is recorded separately from token ownership. The page
  does not imply transfer of copyright or reproduction rights.
- Marketplace rarity and valuation are outside the decision dossier.

## Remaining verification before any later acceptance

The offer and Wave resolution treat the five scheduled tokens as one complete
gift. That is the decision scope; it is not a claim that the token contracts
are technically or legally inseverable.

Later accession processing still records donor identity/address binding and
authority to transfer, legal title,
  encumbrances, complete rights position, preservation permissions, transfer,
  custody receipt, formal acceptance, and accession.

## Release gates

- Governed public candidate record, seven source parts, generated dossier, and
  Storm package validate and enter the deterministic Museum manifest.
- Semantic checks fail on missing or duplicate works, object/image mismatch,
  reordered parts, unsafe paths, stale generated dossiers, status drift, and
  partial chain observations.
- Every Wave part remains below the platform's 25,000-character limit and each
  work part carries its exact source image, alternative text, credit, and
  rights label.
- The donor receives a copy-ready package and submits it as one Storm drop; no
  automation account posts, votes, transfers, or assigns an accession number.
- Repository CI, deterministic manifest checks, and pull-request review gates
  pass before the package is treated as ready for submission.
