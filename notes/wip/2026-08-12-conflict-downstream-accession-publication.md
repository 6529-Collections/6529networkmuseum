- Museum Wave selection is the acquisition decision.
- Rights review, technical review, source fixity, contextual display,
  accessibility, preservation planning, cataloguing, and exhibition are the
  downstream accession work that follows selection.
- The active media-source continuity amendment is required in the atomic
  visitor publication so the frontend can verify each displayed Arweave source
  against the governed work, historical Wave locator, digest, dimensions,
  byte size, and display policy.
- Proposal-time facts remain dated to the proposal observation. Rights and
  technical conclusions produced by downstream accession review use the
  amendment observation time; they must not be backdated before Wave selection.
- This publication correction creates no title, custody, preservation master,
  formal accession, accession number, or permanent Collection membership.
