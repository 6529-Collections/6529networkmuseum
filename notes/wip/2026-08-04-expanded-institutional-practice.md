# Institutional practice study, expanded field

Status: research synthesis and source drafting complete; governed source review
and frontend release pending. This note is not Museum policy, does not rank
institutions, and must not be read as an endorsement list.

## Mandate

Deepen the Museum's comparative study of contemporary and digital-art
institutions. The expanded field gives greater weight to institutions whose
public record demonstrates sustained work with software, networks, computation,
time-based media, digital preservation, or born-digital access. It will also
revisit the publication standard against close readings of exemplary museum
scholarship.

The public result must help the 6529 Network Museum make better records and
exhibitions. Institutional profiles are supporting research, not a prominent
brand display, league table, or substitute for the art in the collection.

## Fixed editorial requirements

- Use primary institutional sources for material institutional claims.
- Distinguish documented practice, the Museum's interpretation, and a proposed
  lesson.
- Ground every lesson in a named work, record, program, conservation case,
  archive, publication, or technical system.
- State where an analogy fails. A building, jurisdiction, endowment, staff
  structure, or custodial practice cannot be imported by rhetoric.
- Study sentences and editorial apparatus as closely as programs and systems.
- Keep institutional comparisons subordinate on the website: discoverable in
  Stories & Research, useful from About and Sources, absent from the collection
  and artwork hierarchy unless a direct scholarly relationship requires it.
- Publish exact sources, research cutoff, version, revision history, and the
  repository contribution path.
- Reject rankings, promotional adjectives, prestige transfer, anonymous
  consensus, and formulaic contrast constructions.
