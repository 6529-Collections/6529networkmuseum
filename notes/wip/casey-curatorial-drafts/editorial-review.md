## Review method

Every manuscript must pass distinct checks for:

1. exact object, title, date, project, accession, rights, and donor facts;
2. claim-to-note support and live source availability;
3. art-historical argument rather than chronological summary;
4. object-specific close looking;
5. technical and medium accuracy;
6. unsupported artist intention or donor motive;
7. reception and counter-pressure;
8. generic or repetitive generated-prose habits;
9. consistency across artist, project, collection, gift, and object layers;
10. publication metadata, links, Markdown integrity, and public-record safety.

Length is not a pass condition.

monograph, collection essay, gift narrative, source matrix, and all seven

Every finding was returned to the original constructor. None of these files
