# Casey Reas generative-system dossier set

## The idea

The seven-work gift is not a sampler of attractive outputs. It is a compact
history of what an image can become inside a generative system: material that
can be cut and rearranged, a surface laid over behavior, a trace left by
sensing machines, a color field rebuilt as space, or a geometric form held
together by memory while it passes through several representations.

The five dossiers explain those transformations closely enough to reconstruct
them, but the point is not to celebrate complexity. It is to make the exact
Museum works more available to looking.

## Shared evidence checkpoint

On 2026-08-04 UTC, read-only retrieval of all seven official generator
responses reproduced the byte lengths and SHA-256 values in the independently
reviewed generator-observation transcript. The response bytes were not added
to this repository. The match supports source-static research against the
currently served generators; it does not close the accession's active software
preservation duty.

The dossiers should be promoted only after independent technical and
curatorial review, rights review of any future annotated code or derived
media, reproducible instrumentation, and explicit resolution or publication
of their material open questions.

## Research status

This is a constructed research package applying the working
[`Generative system analysis standard`](../../../../docs/generative-system-analysis.md)
to all five projects represented by the seven works in accession
`6529NM.2026.001`.

The dossiers are not governed accession amendments, artist-approved accounts,
rights grants, official manifestations, rarity analyses, or claims that the
referenced generator byte streams have been preserved. The reviewed accession
records remain controlling. Material claims distinguish source-static
reconstruction, formal derivation, execution observation, artist statement,
and Museum interpretation.
