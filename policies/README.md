# Policies and governing sources

- `founding-and-operating-principles.md` transcribes the permanent institutional note. It is a founding reference, not represented here as a Wave `WINNER` proposal.
- `general-nft-collecting-scope.md` is the exact adopted collecting-scope proposal.
- [`donation-acceptance.md`](donation-acceptance.md) is the exact adopted donation policy, with Markdown-only heading normalization.
- [`museum-gifts-acquisition-programs-and-funding-assets.md`](museum-gifts-acquisition-programs-and-funding-assets.md) is the exact adopted Museum Gifts, Acquisition Programs, and Funding Assets Policy (`6529NM-POL-004`, decision `6529NM-GOV-1282091`). Its Section 14 amends the donation policy and related decisions only where necessary. The donation policy file is retained unchanged.
- Collection-specific preapprovals are recorded separately in `records/collections/approved-collections.json` and must not be silently folded into the donation policy.

Future amendments retain the prior file and add an explicit superseding policy/decision record.
