# Proposed gift: Sebastião Salgado, *Amazônia*

Six photographs move from the forest floor to the mountain and river systems
above it, then return to two Indigenous communities whose lives cannot be
separated from this land. Together they offer a concentrated view of
Sebastião Salgado's last major project and of the questions carried by his
documentary photography.

Sebastião Salgado made the photographs for *Amazônia*. punk6529 offers tokens
`226`, `1117`, `2059`, `2216`, `2768`, and `4697` as one non-severable group
under the proposed Museum title *Amazônia: Forest, Water, and Community*. The
proposed acquisition is an unrestricted gift at no cost to the Museum.

## Resolution

**Should the 6529 Network Museum select exactly tokens `226`, `1117`, `2059`,
`2216`, `2768`, and `4697` from Ethereum contract
`0xf1f036cebb4b64f138e4f57754edbceb64fb80fc`, presented as the non-severable
group *Amazônia: Forest, Water, and Community*, as a proposed gift for the
permanent Collection?**

If the proposal clears the applicable TDH threshold, the exact six-work group
is selected. Donor authority, legal title, gift terms, rights, technical
review, transfer, custody receipt, condition, preservation, formal acceptance,
and accession will follow. If a required check fails, the gift will not be
accepted.

If the proposal does not clear the threshold, it closes without selection and
has no effect on the Collection. It would not exclude another Salgado work or
another group from *Amazônia* from future consideration.
