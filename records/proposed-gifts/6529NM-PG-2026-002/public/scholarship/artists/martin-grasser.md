# Martin Grasser: artist and collaborator

Martin Grasser works across typography, letterform abstraction, generative systems, and code. In *Themes and Variations*, his contribution is artistic as well as technical: he collaborated with Vera Molnár on the system that positions, scales, weights, distributes, and colours the project’s letterforms.

The project’s N, F, and T forms move between language and geometry. Grasser’s role is visible in the translation of Molnár’s drawn studies into programmable relations, while Molnár’s authorship remains present in the choice of forms, the visual field, and the inquiry into repetition and variation. The Museum credits the work to “Vera Molnár, in collaboration with Martin Grasser” and treats that wording as a creator relation, not as a division between artist and implementer.

Sources: [Martin Grasser, artist profile](https://portraits.martingrasser.com/about/), [Bright Moments interview](https://www.brightmoments.io/quarterly/martin-grasser), and [Sotheby’s project essay](https://www.sothebys.com/en/articles/vera-molnar-the-grande-dame-of-generative-art).
