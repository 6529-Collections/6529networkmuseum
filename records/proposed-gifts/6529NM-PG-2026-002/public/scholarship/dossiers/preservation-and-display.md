# Preservation and display: *Themes and Variations* #210

The work should be shown square, without cropping, with the deep blue and warm white kept in clear contrast. The live generator is the primary manifestation for interpretation because the work is non-static and its renderer belongs to the object’s constitution. The official PNG is a dated documentation surrogate: it is useful for catalogue, accessibility, and stable citation, but it is not the whole work.

Preservation begins with the parts that can be made exact. The Museum retains the token metadata response, official PNG, Wave publication, custody responses, finalized block, transfer receipt, and source manifest. The exact on-chain project scripts and runtime responses are retained in a separate technical package. The custody summary is at `evidence/vera-molnar-210-custody` with SHA-256 `a4f3b10199b566c619c164e352be85072bb71197e2328834ab2d6f2efdd5987b`; the official source manifest is at `evidence/vera-molnar-210-sources` with SHA-256 `71fb6066e1113e22c8586e195bfb8d553dcc8c4d7dec5b33de3ae47d3ebcafe5`.

Art Blocks metadata and generator services may change, the project base URI remains a mutable service layer, and browser rendering can vary with runtime and colour management. The preservation package therefore records dated responses, fixity, contract calls, the exact on-chain script, and the distinction between that script and an image rendered from it. Responsive WebP derivatives are recorded in the accession’s presentation manifest; they are delivery surrogates, not replacements for the original PNG or live renderer.
