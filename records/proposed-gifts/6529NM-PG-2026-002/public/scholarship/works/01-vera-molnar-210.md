# Vera Molnár, in collaboration with Martin Grasser
## *Themes and Variations* #210, 2023

In #210, cobalt blue and warm white exchange the square’s ground and figure. A heavy blue field occupies the upper right, its edge breaking into small steps at the margins; another blue mass fills much of the lower left. Between them, pale fields open and close like panels in a compact diagram.

The lower-left field carries a descending group of white T forms. The letter stays readable while becoming a repeated unit, a measured rhythm rather than a word. A small F at the left edge is equally a glyph and a piece of geometry. Elsewhere, a narrow horizontal rule supports vertical lines, and a dense rectangular panel compresses the image into texture. The eye moves among open field, block, letter, line, and mark.

That movement is the work’s subject. The project’s `F bold` trait names a source condition; the rendered image distributes it across different scales and degrees of legibility. A letter can become a mass, a repeated mark can approach notation, and a grid can loosen into an irregular edge. Molnár’s long interest in rule and deviation remains visible even as the work is generated through a live browser system.

The work is one unique output in a fixed edition of 500. Its exact identity is `eip155:1/erc721:0xe034bb2b1b9471e11cf1a0a9199a156fb227aa5d/210`; its token hash is `0xd0a3be9aa1a3e101a12ec038ceb71a18846dbc62eac3e91fb425232e7820a318`. Published traits are `Grid: 6x6`, `Letters: NFT`, `Vera Letter: F bold`, and palette `b #00188D, b #F3F0E9`.

The official record marks the work `is_static: false` and exposes a live generator. The 2,400 × 2,400 PNG used here is a dated documentation manifestation, retrieved 23 August 2026; the live renderer and its on-chain script remain part of the work’s constitution.
