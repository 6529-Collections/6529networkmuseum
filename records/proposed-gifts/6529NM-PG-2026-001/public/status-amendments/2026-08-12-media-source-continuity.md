## Finding

During real-browser staging review at 07:37:56 UTC, the first photograph did
not render from its historical Wave-upload URL. Chromium reported
`net::ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ORB`. Direct requests to the same CloudFront object were
not consistent with browser delivery, and the response did not provide a
cross-origin access header. The historical URLs remain valid evidence of the
signed Wave publication, but they are not a reliable visitor-display source.

This finding belongs to downstream accession work. It records the technical
review of the five photographs and corrects how the accession publication
delivers them. It does not alter the Wave selection, the five Work identities,
the gift status, or any title, custody, rights, preservation, accession, or
Collection fact.
