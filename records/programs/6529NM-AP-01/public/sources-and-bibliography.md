# Keys and Gates — sources, bibliography, and chronology

This register supports the public corpus. It distinguishes canonical Museum records, direct program submissions, artist-controlled pages, independent/public biographies, and institutional context sources. Web sources were reviewed on `2026-08-08` UTC unless a local record gives a different observation time.

## Source grades

- **A — canonical or submitted evidence:** Museum-retained program/outcome record, direct Wave submission, or artist statement supplied with the selected outcome.
- **B — institutional or independent corroboration:** official site, public institution, scholarly catalogue, or reputable independent profile.
- **C — artist/platform context:** artist website, 6529 profile, Linktree, or platform page useful for public context but not sufficient alone for legal authority.
- **D — identity lead:** weak, indirect, or ambiguous association kept for correspondence only.
- **U — unresolved:** a conflict, missing instrument, or unsupported claim requiring direct review.

## Source gaps requiring artist or registrar action

The major gaps are exact site/date/metadata confirmation; artist authority and preferred credit; executed CC0 instruments; depicted-person, minor, nude, passport, and sensitive-biography consents; graffiti/poster/site/miniature third-party rights; source-file and layered-work retention; and any future mint, purchase, title, custody, preservation, or accession evidence. The registrar work queue turns these gaps into questions without publishing private contact details.
