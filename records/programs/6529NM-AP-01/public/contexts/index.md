# Keys and Gates — context register

These short context notes provide public, source-graded background for selected works. They are not claims that a work documents the whole history of a site, and they do not replace the artist’s supplied caption. A context is included only where a public institutional source supports the limited proposition stated.

## Fort Frederick, Grenada — OUT-004

The Grenada Tourism Authority dates Fort Frederick’s construction to a French start in 1779 and English completion in 1783, and describes its strategic position and later revolutionary use. The site’s military history is relevant to the submitted image’s architecture of confinement and exit. It does not independently verify the artist’s family narrative, the date discrepancy between 2020 and 2021, or the children’s consent status. Sources: [Grenada Tourism Authority](https://www.puregrenada.com/fort-frederick/), [UNESCO tentative-list entry](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/1926/).

## Berlin Wall / *Residual Barrier* — OUT-005

The Berlin Wall Memorial and German Federal Archives provide public histories of the Wall’s construction, border regime, and fall. The submitted photograph is an artist-attributed 2011 image of a weathered section and does not establish a precise location or a direct view of the Wall’s former border line. The work’s concern with a barrier that remains after passage is therefore an interpretive bridge, not a site-identification claim. Sources: [Berlin Wall Memorial](https://www.berlin.de/en/museums/3109351-3104050-gedenkstaette-berliner-mauer.en.html), [German Federal Archives](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/themen-entdecken/online-entdecken/dokumente-zur-zeitgeschichte/bau-der-berliner-mauer/).

## Moraine Lake access — OUT-007

Parks Canada states that Moraine Lake Road is closed to personal vehicles and that access is provided through shuttle and licensed commercial transport arrangements. That public control supports the work’s title, *the cost of open*, without proving the artist’s exact date, route, or access permit. Source: [Parks Canada visitor information](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit/parkbus/moraine-lake).

## RAW-Gelände, Berlin — OUT-009

The official RAW history traces the site from a nineteenth-century railway workshop through railway-repair use, shutdown, temporary cultural occupation, and later redevelopment. This is the evidentiary basis for describing *Now Is Our Time* as a photograph of an industrial site whose cultural access and property regime changed over time. The exact building and rights in the graffiti remain unresolved. Sources: [RAW official history](https://raw-gelaende.de/en/historie/), [RAW English site history](https://raw-gelaende.de/en/about-r-a-w/).

## Casa NUA, São Paulo — OUT-011

Casa NUA’s public materials describe a São Paulo venue for digital-native art, events, production, and cultural programming. That institutional context supports the work’s setting, but not the subject’s sensitive biography, passport details, room permission, or direct consent. Sources: [Casa NUA](https://nua.casa/), [Domínio Público](https://dominiopublico.nua.casa/).

## Bangladesh Liberation War — OUT-015

The Liberation War Museum records the war’s culmination in Bangladesh’s independence on 16 December 1971; the Bangladesh Army’s public history situates the conflict in 1971. The Bangla title invokes that historical frame, but the photograph itself is a contemporary train-window scene and should not be described as documentary evidence of the war. Sources: [Liberation War Museum](https://www.liberationwarmuseumbd.org/page/history), [Bangladesh Army history](https://www.army.mil.bd/history).

## San Roque dam claim — OUT-014

An institutional water-quality publication from Argentina’s Instituto Nacional del Agua refers to the San Roque dam’s 1944 construction phase. This supports only the narrow historical lead that a 1944 dam phase exists in the public record. The artist’s claims about automatic pressure regulation, the exact structure, and the circumstances of capture require direct verification. Source: [INA publication](https://www.ina.gov.ar/archivos/publicaciones/Cirsa_Limno_Hypolimnion_Water_Quality_2004.pdf).

## Contexts intentionally withheld or kept attributed

- *Managed Freedom* (OUT-003): Kayseri and the horse scene are supplied by the artist; no exact site or broader horse-culture history is inferred.
- *Dichotomy.* (OUT-008): the image’s alleged prison/garden relationship remains the artist’s caption; the facility is not identified.
- *Checkpoint* (OUT-010) and *Rusted* (OUT-012): location and political context remain withheld or country-level only for safety.
- *No Access* (OUT-016): the miniature’s material and architectural references are not mapped to a real place.
