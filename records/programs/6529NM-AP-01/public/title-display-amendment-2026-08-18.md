# Title display amendment: *the Artist in the Open Sea*

**Authority:** 6529NM-AP-01-TITLE-DISPLAY-2026-08-18-001  
**Effective:** 18 August 2026  
**Work:** 6529NM-W-0009 / OUT-002  
**Supersedes:** the prior public-display treatment that reproduced the submitted transposition in the Work title and media credit

## Decision

The Museum will display the title as *the Artist in the Open Sea* in visitor-facing pages, labels, captions, and media credits. This is an editorial display correction of the obvious transposition “teh.”

The signed Wave submission and PROGRAM_OUTCOME remain unchanged and retain *the Artist in teh Open Sea* as submitted. The amendment does not claim that the artist has issued a revised title. It keeps the archival source and the Museum’s public display treatment distinct.

## Record effect

- PUBLIC_ENTITY 6529NM-W-0009 uses *the Artist in the Open Sea* as its preferred public label and profile title.
- MEDIA_REFERENCE 6529NM-MED-0021 uses the corrected title in its public credit and preferred label.
- The exact submitted title remains in 6529NM-AP-01-OUT-002, its verbatim artist statement, and the Wave evidence.
- Work identity, selection status, mint status, acquisition status, accession status, Collection membership, rights, and media bytes do not change.

This amendment supplies the authority for the corrected visitor-facing title while preserving the historical record.
