# Nasim Ghanizadeh (nasimghanizadeh)

Nasim Ghanizadeh publicly identifies as a photographer and video artist on the [public Linktree](https://linktr.ee/Nasim_Ghanizadeh), which connects the handle to photography and digital-art platforms. The [Julien Sunyé essay](https://www.juliensunye.com/journal/after-the-gold-rush) describes a practice that combines photography and poetry and includes psychologically charged imagery; the [Bideodromo 2019 catalogue](https://www.bideodromo.com/2019/CATALOGObideodromo2019.pdf) also lists video work. The public sources establish this practice context; fuller biography, legal identity, education, and career history remain open.

In *Managed Freedom*, Ghanizadeh photographs a rider rising on a rearing white horse above a crowded herd in Kayseri, Turkey. The scene’s open horizon is countered by training, hierarchy, and the compressed movement of the animals. The artist connects the image to migration, refusal, shelter, and negotiated freedom. Those themes extend the practice context; the submission’s interpretation remains attributed.
