# Teyhu

Teyhu’s [public Linktree](https://linktr.ee/teyhu) identifies a photographer and connects the handle to a fine-art, 1/1 photographic practice that includes self-portraiture. No reliable independent biography, legal name, city, or exhibition history has been verified.

In *Rusted*, Teyhu places a distant figure with keys inside a sequence of damaged rooms. Broken plaster, exposed brick, arched windows, rubble, and shadow turn a building into a system of exhausted passages. The submission identifies the image as a self-portrait made in Iran and describes the keys as rusted promises of entry; the work’s formal attention to ruin and distance carries that reading without identifying the site.
