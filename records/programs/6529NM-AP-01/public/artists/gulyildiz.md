# Gül Yıldız (GulYildiz)

Gül Yıldız is a photographer and multidisciplinary artist whose public professional practice also includes curation, cinema writing, art-magazine writing, teaching, and judging. The [Fujifilm public profile](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/en-gb/photographers/gul-yildiz/) describes photography and videography training, an engineering background, AFIAP recognition, Fujifilm X-Photographer affiliation, and the Artrospektif Photography Atelier. That profile places her in Istanbul; the selected photograph was submitted from Cologne.

Her photographic language is attentive to architecture, framing, filtered light, and the way a human figure is scaled by a built environment. In *Take the Key!*, a solitary figure stands before a patterned blue screen inside a stone hall. The screen turns daylight into a sequence of bands and makes the threshold a problem of visibility before it becomes a question of movement. The artist connects the image to knowledge, authority, digital sovereignty, Web3 ownership, and self-custody; those meanings remain part of the artist’s account.
