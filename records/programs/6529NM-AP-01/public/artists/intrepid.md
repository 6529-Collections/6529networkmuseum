# intrepid

The selected artist works publicly under the pseudonym intrepid. An artist-attributed first-person photo essay under intrepidphotos describes an Australian family stranded on a sailing vessel during the pandemic and later admitted to Grenada. The account aligns closely with the narrative supplied for *No Key, Only Light*, but the public record does not establish a legal identity or a fuller biography.

The artist’s practice, as represented by this work, is documentary family photography shaped by travel, confinement, and the charged architecture of a historic site. Two silhouetted young figures move through Fort Frederick toward a blown-out opening of daylight. The image holds a private return to land against the fort’s public histories of defence and revolution; the children’s partial anonymity is part of the photograph’s restraint.

**Further research:** Intrepid remains the safe public credit; the account relationship, date, figures’ privacy boundary, and guardian-consent scope remain to be confirmed.
