# Artem Humilevskiy (GIANT)

Public artist sources use Artem Humilevskiy for the Ukrainian photographer associated with the artist identity GIANT. His [artist site](https://humilevskiy.com/), the [Global Peace Photo Award profile](https://globalpeacephotoaward.org/submissions/winners-2022/giant), and [Galerie Sechs](https://www.galeriesechs.ch/artists-featured/artem-humilevskiy) place him in Mykolaiv, identify a 1986 birth year, and discuss the pandemic-era *Giant* self-portrait project. The submitted statement spells the name Artem Humilevskyi; the Museum preserves that spelling as raw source text and uses Humilevskiy for this public profile pending direct confirmation. The project addresses body image, vulnerability, self-acceptance, prejudice, and resilience through the artist’s own body and domestic environments.

In *The Hostile Gate*, Humilevskiy opens a refrigerator while his shirtless body fills the foreground. The warm interior light and colourful magnets make the appliance familiar, but its sparse contents and the force of the gesture turn it into a threshold between appetite, scarcity, and bodily judgement. The image belongs to the project’s direct self-portrait language: the domestic object is a gate, and the artist’s body is both witness and subject.
