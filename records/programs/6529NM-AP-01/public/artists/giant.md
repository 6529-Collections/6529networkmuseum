# Artem Humilevskyi (GIANT)

Artem Humilevskyi is a Ukrainian photographer associated publicly with the artist identity GIANT. His [artist site](https://humilevskiy.com/), the [Global Peace Photo Award profile](https://globalpeacephotoaward.org/submissions/winners-2022/giant), and profiles at [Spilne Art](https://spilne.art/en/artist/artem-humilevskiy/) and [Galerie Sechs](https://www.galeriesechs.ch/artists-featured/artem-humilevskiy) place him in Mykolaiv, identify a 1986 birth year, and discuss the pandemic-era *Giant* self-portrait project. That project addresses body image, vulnerability, self-acceptance, prejudice, and resilience through the artist’s own body and domestic environments.

In *The Hostile Gate*, Humilevskyi opens a refrigerator while his shirtless body fills the foreground. The warm interior light and colourful magnets make the appliance familiar, but its sparse contents and the force of the gesture turn it into a threshold between appetite, scarcity, and bodily judgement. The image belongs to the project’s direct self-portrait language: the domestic object is a gate, and the artist’s body is both witness and subject.
