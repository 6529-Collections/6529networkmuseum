# arsonic

**Publication note:** Concise artist note based on public handle sources and the selected submission; photographic authority remains open.

Arsonic is a public pseudonymous artist and builder associated with Web3, meme-oriented, and generative-art communities. The artist’s [public X profile](https://x.com/Ars0nic) and [6529 profile](https://6529.io/arsonic/xtdh) establish that digital-art context; they do not establish a conventional photography biography or a legal identity. The Museum keeps the handle as the public credit.

In *Nowhere To Esc.*, Arsonic translates a software command into a tabletop still life. Black keyboard keys spell “NO / WHERE / TO,” with an Esc key separated below and a small ant beside it. The work’s conceptual force comes from the mismatch between a clearly labelled exit and a subject too small to use it. The image extends a digital practice into physical scale, language, and staged photographic comedy.
