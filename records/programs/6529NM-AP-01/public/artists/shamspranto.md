# Shams Pranto (shamspranto)

Shams Pranto is described as a Bangladeshi photographer who uses photography to tell stories about Bangladesh’s land, people, and everyday life in the [Get Bengal photo essay](https://www.getbengal.com/photo/details/the-story-of-bangladesh-and-lonely-life-through-shams-photography). The [6529 profile](https://6529.io/shamspranto) says the artist is currently staying in India. The [121Clicks profile](https://121clicks.com/photo-stories/shams-pranto-bangladesh-photography/) identifies Shams Nayeem Pranto and describes a Bangladesh-focused practice involving ordinary life, solitude, and social observation; the bridge between those public names and the selected handle remains to be confirmed.

In *মুক্তিযুদ্ধ - Fight for Freedom*, two women lean from separate windows of a weathered teal train. Patterned and orange head coverings, partial faces, the carriage wall, and a torn poster create a layered image of transit and historical memory. The title invokes the 1971 Liberation War, while the photograph remains a contemporary collage of people and a train. The artist identifies one depicted woman as the artist’s mother; consent and poster provenance remain on the work’s rights record.
