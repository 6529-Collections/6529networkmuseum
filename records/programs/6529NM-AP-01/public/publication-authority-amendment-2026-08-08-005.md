# Keys and Gates — limited editorial-display authority amendment

**Record ID:** `6529NM-AP-01-PUBLICATION-AUTHORITY-2026-08-08-005`

**Record type:** append-only derived publication-authority amendment
**Revision:** 1
**Issued:** 8 August 2026
**Constructor:** 6529 Network Museum, Curatorial Research and Registrar
**Scope:** all sixteen selected Keys and Gates outcomes, current provisional editorial edition

This amendment records the completed registrar disposition for the current
limited public catalogue. It is an authority record for a defined editorial
display, not a rights grant, acquisition act, accession, or Collection record.
It corrects the publication-layer contradiction between the retained program
and submission evidence, which supports a public catalogue, and the earlier
qualified wording that treated the presentation authorization as unresolved.
It does not supersede any selected-outcome source record, historical
assertion, `rights_effective_status`, or independent acquisition gate.

## Determination

The authority for all sixteen outcomes is:

`PROVISIONAL_EDITORIAL_DISPLAY_LIMITED`

The determination rests on the retained evidence that:

1. the program record's `publication_outputs` names a curatorial subcollection
   page, individual standardized object pages, and a compact downloadable
   catalogue;
2. the sixteen outcome records preserve signed `WINNER` selection evidence
   from the program Wave; and
3. the verbatim submission records preserve the submitting artist's rights
   assertion (including conditional `CC0`/`CCO` wording) and, where people are
   depicted, the program's consent-documentation representation.

The source trail is explicit: the [program record](../program.json) supplies
the publication outputs and submission terms; the [selected-work index](../selected-works.json)
supplies the sixteen selected aliases; and the [outcome records](../outcomes/)
retain the signed selection evidence, verbatim artist statements, and
submission-level rights/consent assertions. The [program terms](../../../../docs/programs/keys-and-gates.md)
define the catalogue and acquisition boundary used by this determination.

The effective scope is limited to the declared Museum-created WebP
presentation surrogates and Museum-authored catalogue text for the current
provisional editorial edition. The scope includes the visual description,
caption, close looking, and source-graded context that the Museum authored for
that edition.

This determination does **not** activate CC0, grant unrestricted reuse,
authorize source or high-resolution publication, establish subject/site/
third-party clearance, or create evidence of mint, purchase, title, custody,
acquisition, accession, or Collection membership. The outcome
`rights_effective_status` remains controlling and unchanged for every work.

The submissions represent that documentation exists or is available on
request where applicable. No consent instrument or other private source
instrument is retained in the Museum record. If a future document is
received, it would be handled in a restricted registrar layer; this completed
display determination is based on the public program and submission evidence
retained now.

## Independent rights dimensions

The current public edition continues to separate:

- artist authorship and conditional copyright dedication;
- depicted-person privacy, dignity, publicity, and consent;
- site, venue, poster, graffiti, document, and third-party-material status;
- source/high-resolution and preservation treatment; and
- future mint, purchase, title, custody, acquisition, accession, and Collection
  membership.

Each dimension remains at its evidence-backed source status. The limited
display authority is positive for the defined catalogue surface while those
dimensions remain independently unresolved where the source record says so.
It is not a blanket model-release rule: the image-specific public derivative,
the program terms, the artist's representation, and the concrete privacy or
dignity risk govern each work's treatment.
