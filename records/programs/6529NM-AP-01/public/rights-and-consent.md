# Keys and Gates — rights and consent boundary

This register is a public review aid, not a rights grant. It records what the selected submissions say, what the reviewed presentation images show, and which permissions remain to be confirmed. The program requires CC0 and written consent for depicted people where applicable. A conditional submission declaration is evidence of an artist’s assertion; it is not an executed Museum instrument.

Creative Commons explains that a CC0 dedication addresses copyright while personality, privacy, publicity, and trademark rights may remain. The same separation applies to site permissions, graffiti, posters, passports, physical miniatures, and third-party materials. See the [CC0 and NFTs FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/cc-and-nfts/) and the [institutional record](institutional-record.md).

## Publication layers

| Layer | Current treatment |
|---|---|
| Public scholarship | Artist handle, source-graded public biography, selected-work metadata, artist-attributed caption, close looking, context links, current media join, and explicit unknowns. |
| Public presentation | Existing WebP derivatives may be referenced as program presentation surrogates under the current media profile; their presence does not activate reuse rights. |
| Restricted registrar file | Legal identity and authority documents, consent/model releases, sensitive locations, unredacted passport/document details, original high-resolution source, layered files, transaction/title/custody evidence, and preservation packages. |
| Unresolved | Any use that requires a rights instrument, depicted-person consent, site permission, third-party clearance, or acquisition/accession evidence not currently retained. |

## Current public-safe wording

The public corpus may say that the sixteen works were selected, that each has a source record and a deterministic presentation join, and that the artist supplied the statements and declarations recorded on each work page. It may describe visible people as they appear in the reviewed derivative without adding names, ages, sensitive biography, or consent conclusions. It may link to public artist and institutional sources while grading them and preserving unresolved identity bridges.

The corpus should use “conditional CC0 declaration,” “artist represents,” “consent status requires review,” “location withheld,” or “source-level verification pending” where those are the evidence-backed facts. It should not use “cleared,” “licensed,” “released,” “public domain,” “Museum-owned,” or “rights-free” for a selected outcome without an executed instrument and registrar determination.

## Open rights questions

Before acquisition review, the registrar must resolve depicted-person consent and safe-publication boundaries for OUT-004, OUT-010, OUT-011, and OUT-015; drone, site, graffiti, poster, miniature, and staged-scene permissions for the relevant works; and the exact CC0 instrument where the submission uses conditional or CCO wording. Direct artist questions and restricted documents belong in the registrar work queue and are not exposed in this public corpus.
