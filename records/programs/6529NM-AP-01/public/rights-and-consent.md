# Keys and Gates — rights and consent boundary

This register is a public review aid, not a rights grant. It records what the selected submissions say, what the current presentation images show, and which permissions remain to be confirmed. The program terms call for CC0 and written consent for depicted people where applicable. This register applies that requirement with an image-specific ethics review: an artist statement of consent is evidence of an assertion, not an executed Museum instrument. A selected artwork may be shown in a provisional public catalogue while sensitive biography, identifiers, high-resolution detail, or unrestricted reuse remain limited pending their own review.

Creative Commons explains that a CC0 dedication addresses copyright while personality, privacy, publicity, and trademark rights may remain. The same separation applies to site permissions, graffiti, posters, passports, physical miniatures, and third-party materials. See the [CC0 and NFTs FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/cc-and-nfts/) and the [institutional record](institutional-record.md).

## Presentation authority boundary

The program publication and retained public Wave submissions establish the source context for a provisional editorial catalogue. The Museum-created 640, 1280, and 2400 WebP files are responsive presentation surrogates, not source originals or high-resolution preservation masters. Their public presentation does not activate the submissions’ conditional CC0 language, grant unrestricted reuse, or determine future on-chain rights. CDN/public presentation authorization remains unresolved for registrar disposition; source originals are not exposed. OUT-011 is limited to its 640 surrogate in this edition because the larger local derivative makes the booklet cover more legible. The program terms, source/display distinction, and transform controls are recorded in [`docs/programs/keys-and-gates.md`](../../../../docs/programs/keys-and-gates.md), [`docs/program-media-delivery.md`](../../../../docs/program-media-delivery.md), and the [institutional record](institutional-record.md).

## Current public-safe wording

The public corpus may say that the sixteen works were selected, that each has a source record and a deterministic presentation join, and that the artist supplied the statements and declarations recorded on each work page. It may describe visible people as they appear in the current derivative without adding names, ages, sensitive biography, or consent conclusions. It may link to public artist and institutional sources while grading them and preserving unresolved identity bridges.

The corpus should use “conditional CC0 declaration,” “artist represents,” “consent status requires review,” “location withheld,” or “source-level verification pending” where those are the evidence-backed facts. It should not use “cleared,” “licensed,” “released,” “public domain,” “Museum-owned,” or “rights-free” for a selected outcome without an executed instrument and registrar determination.

## Display-specific ethical basis

The current selected-work derivatives may be shown as provisional editorial/art display because the program publication includes a catalogue and the submissions identify the artists and assert authorship or conditional rights. The 640/1280/2400 files are Museum-created presentation surrogates with per-work public-size controls; they are not source originals, preservation masters, or unrestricted-reuse grants. That basis supports presentation of the artwork as an artwork while each depicted person, venue, poster, document, miniature component, and source file remains subject to its own review. For sensitive works, the Museum limits the public derivative, suppresses unnecessary biography and identifiers, adds content notices, and keeps the high-resolution/source treatment subject to registrar review.

## Open rights questions

Before acquisition review, the registrar should resolve the image-specific boundaries for OUT-004, OUT-010, OUT-011, and OUT-015; drone, site, graffiti, poster, miniature, and staged-scene permissions for the relevant works; and the exact CC0 instrument where the submission uses conditional or CCO wording. OUT-004 requires confirmation of the represented consent coverage and safe wording for the distant silhouettes; the public derivative does not establish that the figures are minors. OUT-010 requires a self-portrait or subject-authority determination. OUT-011 requires direct subject approval for the nude display and sensitive biography, a document-legibility check at the approved size, and venue review. OUT-015 requires consent-scope and poster/collage review for both visible people. These are concrete publication and acquisition questions, not a blanket release doctrine. Direct artist questions and restricted documents belong in the registrar work queue and are not exposed in this public corpus.
