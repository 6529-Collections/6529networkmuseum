# Title, rights, and accession review

**Accession lot:** `6529NM.2026.001`
**Decision date:** 2026-08-02
**Acquisition method:** gift
**Donor credit:** punk6529
**Objects reviewed:** seven Casey REAS Art Blocks tokens, `6529NM.2026.001.01` through `.07`
**Institutional author:** 6529 Network Museum, Registrar
**Decision authority:** direct Museum-authorized collection authority recorded in `6529NM.2026.001.GAA-01`

## Determination

The 6529 Network Museum determines that the seven scheduled tokens are eligible for and accepted into the permanent collection. The donor made a full gift: the Museum accepts the donor's entire ownership interest in the tokens and every donor-held interest that is transferable with them, without consideration and without a retained donor interest. The common Ethereum transfer into `networkmuseum.6529.eth`, the exact contract/token schedule, the Museum's formal gift acceptance, and this reviewed title declaration together establish a sufficient title basis for accession.

This determination does not collapse token ownership into copyright ownership. The Museum owns the seven tokenized collection objects. No copyright assignment from Casey REAS is asserted or inferred. Copyright and similar rights remain with the applicable licensor or rights holder except to the extent exercised under the public license attached to each work's retained Art Blocks metadata.

The Museum accepts the ordinary residual risk that a public blockchain cannot disclose every possible private claim, security interest, incapacity, or off-chain dispute. No contrary claim, competing on-chain owner, consideration, donor reservation, or object-identity conflict is present in the reviewed record. That residual limitation is documented and accepted; it is not an open accession gate.

## Evidence and title analysis

The title conclusion rests on four mutually reinforcing records:

1. The donor's instruction, treated by the Museum as a full and intentional gift of all donor-held interests in the seven tokens.
2. The formally accepted [Gift Acceptance and Accession Authorization](gift-acceptance-authorization.md), made under the adopted Art Blocks preapproval and Donation Acceptance Policy.
3. Ethereum transaction `0xbdde33b32d4b70335b10cbd37c0b00a027844f14c900d82aa4f75b7a7b390498`, whose seven ERC-721 `Transfer` logs deliver the exact scheduled assets to the Museum custody address.
4. The object-level chain identities, transaction log indices, and custody findings in the machine records and retained raw RPC receipt.

The transfer transaction is both custody evidence and performance of the gift. The institutional title declaration does not pretend to be a donor-signed deed; it records the Museum's reviewed conclusion from the donor's full-gift declaration, completed delivery, formal acceptance, and exact object schedule. The Museum considers that evidence sufficient for these no-consideration token gifts under its donation pathway.

The provenance record is complete for the accession event and exact receipt. Earlier token history may be enriched through continuing scholarship, but the absence of a prose ownership history for every prior wallet is not an accession blocker where the object identity, donor gift, delivery, and current custody are verified and no adverse claim is known.

## Copyright and public-license analysis

All seven retained Art Blocks metadata responses identify the artist as Casey REAS and state `CC BY-NC 4.0`. The Museum treats those labels as object-specific source evidence that the licensed material is offered under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International Public License. Under the [official legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en), the license grants worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable permission to reproduce and share the licensed material and to produce, reproduce, and share adapted material for noncommercial purposes. It permits all media and formats and technical modifications needed to exercise those rights. The [official deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/deed.en) summarizes the same permissions and principal conditions.

The Museum therefore makes the following affirmative use determinations:

## Attribution and use protocol

Whenever the Museum shares a work or an adapted version, the accompanying credit must, so far as supplied and reasonably practicable:

- identify Casey REAS as creator and preserve any other supplied attribution party;
- identify the work and its Museum accession number;
- state `CC BY-NC 4.0` and link to `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/`;
- link to the source object or official project record where reasonably practicable;
- retain supplied copyright and warranty-disclaimer notices;
- state whether the Museum modified the material and preserve notice of earlier modifications; and
- avoid any suggestion that Casey REAS, Art Blocks, or another attribution party endorses the Museum or a particular use.

Recommended collection credit: `Casey REAS, [title], [year]; on-chain generative software associated with an Ethereum ERC-721 token; 6529 Network Museum, gift of punk6529, [accession number]. Licensed CC BY-NC 4.0.` Add `Museum documentation capture` or a precise change statement to derivatives and surrogates.

## Registrar and risk conclusions

- **Identity and authenticity:** pass. Exact CAIP-19 identities, token hashes where supplied, Art Blocks project data, raw metadata bytes, and the common transfer receipt are fixed in the evidence package.
- **Donor authority and title:** pass with accepted residual risk. The Museum relies on the donor's full-gift declaration and completed delivery; no adverse claim or donor reservation is recorded.
- **Provenance:** pass for accession. The accession transfer is fully documented in the Stream-compatible provenance schedule; earlier chain history remains ordinary cataloguing enrichment.
- **Rights:** pass with conditions. The Museum owns the tokens, not the artist's copyright; the object-specific CC BY-NC 4.0 grant supports the noncommercial museum uses listed above.
- **Compliance:** pass. The gift involved no consideration, no restricted material, no personal-data payload, and no identified sanctions, dispute, or encumbrance indicator in the reviewed record. The limits of public wallet evidence are accepted rather than mislabeled as certainty.
- **Curatorial case:** pass. The artist profile, collection essay, and object records establish the significance of the artist, the projects, the seven works, and the collection's fit with the Museum's mission without converting interpretation into artist intent.

## Accession outcome

Title, rights, identity, provenance, curatorial significance, condition, and technical evidence are sufficient for accession. The lot is approved for the permanent collection as `accessioned`. Long-term software preservation, dependency capture, multi-environment rendering, periodic custody verification, and future provenance enrichment remain active stewardship duties. They do not postpone the accession decision.
