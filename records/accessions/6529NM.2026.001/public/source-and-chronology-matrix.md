### Publication-safe summary

Punk6529 gave the 6529 Network Museum seven already-minted Art Blocks works by Casey Reas. The Museum accepted the full gift on 1 August 2026 and accessioned the seven works into the permanent collection on 2 August 2026. The Museum owns the tokens and the donor's transferable interest in them; copyright remains separate. The retained metadata identifies CC BY-NC 4.0, and the accession carries active software-preservation obligations.

### Claims not supported by the record

- that Casey Reas selected or endorsed the seven-work group;
- that the donor described a personal selection thesis or motive beyond making the full gift;
- that the three CENTURY works form an artist-designated triptych or suite;
- that the group is a retrospective, complete survey, or canonical selection;
- that token title includes copyright;
- that accession completed autonomous preservation;
- that any work was donated because of rarity, market value, or investment potential;
- that a static Art Blocks image is the complete artwork.

### Retention boundary

The initial official static PNG responses and two full-viewport screenshots for each object were referenced and hash-recorded as time-specific documentation surrogates. Their bytes were not retained in the initial public package. The [visual-observation record](../../../../records/accessions/6529NM.2026.001/visual-observation-record.json) leaves a new, attributed preservation recapture open; that future capture must be retained as a separately dated object and must not be represented as the original observation bytes. A platform thumbnail is a platform-authorized documentation state. It is never a Museum-declared canonical state unless a controlling artist, contract, or accession record explicitly gives it that status. For *Phototaxis*, the precise supported term is **the official 1,000-iteration thumbnail state**.

## 5. Shared 2026-08-02 close-looking baseline

These observations were made from the exact official token stills at original resolution. They are a common baseline, not language to copy unchanged into every essay.

| Object | Formal baseline | Questions for the object writer |
|---|---|---|
| CENTURY #31 | Circular field dominated by slate, saturated blue, and cream. Dense vertical slices interrupt diagonal cream and pale-salmon lines. Large curved cream forms are partly buried by the slicing structure. | How do the near-central splices change scale and continuity? Does the tondo-like boundary stabilize or intensify the fractured interior? What changes after interactive recomposition? |
| CENTURY #724 | Broad cream field with rust-red arcs and upper bands. Sparse charcoal and salmon diagonals cross large negative areas; a dark lower-right crescent and isolated lower verticals hold the open field. | How does openness alter the project's characteristic density? Does the work feel suspended, cut apart, or unfinished in a productive sense? How does Janky manifest without using the label as explanation? |
| CENTURY #401 | Gray field, black vertical columns, white diagonals, and translucent circular or arc forms. Slices read as both surface interruption and shallow architectural depth. | How do opacity and grayscale change the relation between slicing and motion? Which planes appear in front, and why does depth remain unstable? |
| Pre-Process #63 | Panoramic black-and-white composition in three horizontal registers. Dark circular masses attach to axial bands while repeated translucent arcs record movement, collision, and accumulation. | How does the 1.78 ratio make this different from a square field? How do the repeated axes discipline the apparent turbulence? What does the official static output disclose, and what changes during execution? |
| Phototaxis #308 | Pale cyan and gray trajectories produce an outer elliptical circulation around a dense lower-central knot, with a second convergence above and long escaping paths. | How does the official 1,000-iteration thumbnail state convert duration into density, curvature, and reach? How do three light positions organize the image without being visible? What changes as execution continues? |
| 923 EMPTY ROOMS #713 | Acid-green diagonal raster crosses a charcoal field but breaks around voids that imply a cornered room. A luminous right seam and bright lower ledge produce simultaneous recession and optical vibration. | Which elements correspond to the exact `555536` colorform sequence? How does the room emerge from absence rather than outline? What does the diagram toggle reveal? |
| Ex Nihilo (Cosmos) #248 | Segmented white traces cross black in unstable polygonal and dodecahedral suggestions. Interruption and scale change create layered depth without a settled solid. | How does a continuous non-repeating display change the meaning of this still? How do channel, speed, pause, and regeneration controls affect the encounter? How should the documented R/B control discrepancy be presented? |

The system originates in 2003, appeared in unresolved form in 2005, and was completed/released as a 120-work Art Blocks project in 2022. Do not date the tokenized work simply “2003,” and do not erase its archaeology by calling it only a 2022 project.

## 10. Critical argument bank

These are propositions to test, not conclusions to repeat automatically.

1. Reas's enduring subject is the interval between instruction and event.
2. The visible image is often a history of relations rather than a picture of an object.
3. His authorship lies in constructing possibility spaces and exclusions, not withdrawing from decision.
4. Physical prints and drawings do not merely reproduce software; they disclose resolution, density, duration, and material trace differently.
5. Processing and Feral File matter because they extend studio values—legibility, iteration, access, peer exchange—into shared infrastructures, but they are not interchangeable with the artworks.
6. The move into blockchain is continuous with earlier questions but materially changes edition, instantiation, distribution, ownership, and public comparison.
7. Transparency of rule can coexist with perceptual opacity; viewers may know the system and still confront fatigue or illegibility.
8. Reas's work has repeatedly moved between endogenous systems and source-image systems: artificial agents and Process on one side; broadcast, cinema, photography, and ML datasets on the other.
9. The seven-work Museum group is strongest as a longitudinal argument built from recent tokens that reach backward into older systems, not as a survey of all media.
10. The gift's institutional significance lies in transferring a coherent study group into public trust and accepting its preservation obligations, not in market status.
