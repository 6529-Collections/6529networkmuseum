# The executable image: rule, behavior, room, cosmos

**Collection-level curatorial essay for lot `6529NM.2026.001`**

This seven-work group entered the permanent collection through a formally accepted full gift and the completed Museum accession certificate `6529NM-ACC-2026-001`. The certificate joins exact title and custody bindings to the reviewed rights, curatorial, and technical determinations. Autonomous software preservation remains in progress, and display is ready with conditions; those continuing duties do not reopen the accession decision.

**Museum interpretation [E]:** The collection proposes an encounter with an executable image: an image whose identity cannot be reduced to a token, a screenshot, or a file because it is formed by instructions carried out over time. This executable-image ontology is the Museum's curatorial frame, not a sourced artist or platform fact. Across CENTURY, Pre-Process, Phototaxis, 923 EMPTY ROOMS, and Ex Nihilo (Cosmos), the frame attends to how systems become legible as color, line, motion, density, and space. The group is bounded rather than comprehensive: three CENTURY works supply comparative states, not a triptych; five project lines do not constitute a retrospective.

## Rule

The three CENTURY states begin with cutting and recomposition. Reas's artist/platform statements connect the project to Ellsworth Kelly and describe a moving system of slices, line, palette, and chance. The Museum's dated observations distinguish each token's static visual surrogate from its live behavior: dark blue/cream circular fragments and vertical divisions (#31), an open rust/cream field with dark partitions (#724), and a grayscale field of black bands, gray planes, and intersecting white lines (#401). These are not claims about intended meaning; they give the comparative sample a precise formal vocabulary.

*Pre-Process* shifts the rule from recomposition to behavior. Its 120 permutations arise from eight surfaces, three origins, and five growth configurations. For #63, rows of circular masses, repeated axes, and translucent sweeps make visible a system of elements whose movement is described by the artist/platform as approaching without reaching equilibrium. The historical point is not that blockchain begins this practice: the work's 2003 origin and relation to Process make the tokenized edition a later instantiation of an ongoing software grammar.

## Behavior

Phototaxis turns paths into traces. Its source record describes simple machines in a light field and situates the code in a lineage running through MicroImage, Braitenberg, Processing, and p5.js. In the static #308 surrogate, the Museum observed a blue-gray central/lower knot with trajectories rising and dispersing. The observation gains its importance from the behavior behind it, but does not purport to identify a particular agent or to replace the artist's own account. The record uses 2021 for the Art Blocks release while retaining the unresolved 2022 date in the current Reas NFT register.

## Room

923 EMPTY ROOMS transfers system logic into a distributed exhibition history. Bright Moments documents six colorforms, six cities, 923 combinations, and—separately—an edition size of 924; Art Blocks likewise describes 923 rooms and a fixed edition of 924 artworks. Neither primary source explains the relationship of the 924th artwork to the 923 rooms, and the Museum does not invent one. The #713 surrogate presents a bright green/dark room-like perspectival field. This reading does not translate the code, city, or project labels into hidden meanings. Nor does it make an individual token an LACMA commission: LACMA provides institutional context for *An Empty Room*, while 923 EMPTY ROOMS is a later Art Blocks × Bright Moments project.

## Cosmos

Ex Nihilo (Cosmos) moves the vocabulary of units and fields into Still Life's dodecahedral/pixel/line system. The #248 static surrogate is a black field with granular white lines and unstable polygonal/dodecahedral suggestions. Art Blocks describes a continuous, non-repeating work with viewer controls; this makes a static capture a time-specific documentation surrogate, never the complete manifestation. The Museum does not infer meanings from project labels such as Janky, Oculi, CHUNK, or # COSMOS beyond exact source and script evidence retained in the dossier.

## A collection of changing states

The controlled [visual-observation record](../visual-observation-record.json) fixes the source URLs, response and screenshot hashes, byte sizes, viewport/canvas geometry, and per-object completion proxies. For every official generator, two full-viewport screenshots taken with a commanded minimum wait of 1500 milliseconds had different hashes. The supplied live timestamp was recorded only after both screenshots and hashing; exact per-frame times and elapsed duration are unavailable. Each official static PNG timestamp is likewise the downloaded file's `LastWriteTimeUtc`, an observation-completion proxy rather than a server or request timestamp. The original PNG and screenshot bytes were not retained. CC BY-NC 4.0 now clears an attributed replacement preservation capture, which must be dated as a later observation rather than represented as the original bytes. These bounded observations support the reviewed amber technical condition; they do not prove determinism, cross-environment reproducibility, autonomous preservation, or unconditional display readiness.

The permanent-collection intent of the gift is thus joined to a stewardship obligation. Art Blocks stores project scripts and token hashes on-chain, and its on-chain generator can assemble many flagship projects without a centralized server; p5.js 1.0.0, reported by six records in this lot, is among the libraries held in the Art Blocks Dependency Registry. Those durable inputs do not eliminate browser, rendering, interaction, timing, or display-environment dependencies, and Ex Nihilo's observed p5.js 1.9.0 route requires separate verification. The Museum will need to retain the relation among chain identity, source code, dependency form, generator assembly, live behavior, still documentation, and public interpretation—not simply accumulate seven images. No marketplace metric, rarity score, aesthetic ranking, quality conclusion, or value claim is used in this essay.
