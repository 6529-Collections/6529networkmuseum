# Web presentation of *Conflict at Its Edges*

The 6529 Network Museum acquired the five tokenized photographs in *Conflict at Its Edges* and interprets that acquisition as carrying the ordinary institutional right to display and publish the works in a credited Museum context. Copyright remains with the photographers and Magnum Photos. The Museum does not claim copyright ownership or a general licence to reproduce, sell, sublicense, download, alter, or train models on the images.

For a practical and accessible web presentation, the Museum may produce proportion-preserving delivery copies from each fixity-verified source image. The approved technical process applies EXIF orientation, converts embedded colour profiles to sRGB or treats an untagged source as sRGB, resizes with Lanczos filtering, and encodes WebP copies at 640, 1280, and 2400 pixels wide. It does not crop, watermark, retouch, recolour, or otherwise alter the depicted content.

Within this accession context, this authority supersedes the proposal-stage delivery rule that barred Museum responsive variants. That earlier rule remains part of the proposal's historical record; this authority does not alter the recorded source objects or their underlying rights.

The exact Arweave image remains the recorded source object. Every web copy is identified by its source hash, transform profile, dimensions, byte size, and its own SHA-256 digest. The browser receives the smallest appropriate copy for its viewport; the source original is not the default page payload. These delivery copies are display surrogates, not preservation masters and not downloadable publication assets.

Each display must retain the work's artist, title, date or place where recorded, Magnum credit, All Rights Reserved notice, and accession context. This authority applies to the Museum's own public site and records. It creates no permission for third-party reuse.
