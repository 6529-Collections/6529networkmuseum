# Web presentation authority: six photographs from *Amazônia*

The Museum may display the six accessioned photographs in its collection,
exhibition, catalogue and educational work under the recorded
[gift and Museum-use determination](title-rights-and-display.md). Present each
photograph uncropped, with its source caption and year, the photographer's
name, and the credit:

> Gift of punk6529. Photograph © Sebastião Salgado.

The six-work gift does not require the photographs to be displayed together.
Copyright remains separate from ownership of the donated tokens. This display
authority grants no general third-party reuse licence.

The Museum's web copies retain the complete framing and orientation. The
documented process converts the embedded Adobe RGB (1998) profile to fixed
sRGB, resizes with Lanczos filtering, and encodes WebP at 640, 1,280 and 2,400
pixels wide. It removes EXIF and XMP metadata and makes no crop, retouching,
watermark or change to the depicted content. The original JPEGs remain in
restricted preservation storage.

The [presentation manifest](presentation-manifest.json) binds every copy to
its Work identifier, source-image hash, dimensions, byte size, transform,
accessibility description and immutable delivery path. It republishes the
exact eighteen derivatives examined in the independent accession review;
this integration creates no new image transformation. Approval of the
publication manifest and activation of its delivery are recorded separately.

This document applies the existing gift determination to the Museum's
established accession media contract. The earlier
[display preparation and preservation account](display-and-preservation.md)
and the [retained preparation record](../../../../evidence/salgado-amazonia-preservation/display-preparation.json)
remain evidence of the original construction. The source originals are not
offered as public master downloads.
