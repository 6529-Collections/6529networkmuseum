## Amendments to proposal language

The original published proposal is preserved. This catalogue describes
token 4697 as a foreground figure with people in a clearing; the source
caption does not establish the occasion as a procession. The initial
September 13 construction draft also mistakenly described reflections in
token 226. Examination of the verified display copy corrected that reading
to dense forest vegetation. The correction is propagated through the group,
artist and project texts. The selected objects and source bytes are unchanged.

No marketplace rarity measure informs the selection or the catalogue.
