# Registration diligence

**Scope:** Six Amazônia photographs, lot 6529NM.2026.004.  
**Prepared:** 13 September 2026; independent intake review approved.

The donor's unrestricted-gift declaration, the exact proposal and the
completed delivery supply the Museum's title and acceptance basis. The
[title record](title-rights-and-display.md) binds that declaration to the six
tokens and their single donor-to-Museum transfer. No consideration or
continuing donor condition is recorded. No further donor questionnaire is
required for the documented Museum uses.

## Control and encumbrance assessment

The retained finalized-block reads show Museum ownership and zero
token-specific approvals for all six objects. The receipt-verified indexed
history joins each mint to the Museum transfer. The source files, token
identities and donor instruction agree on the object schedule. No adverse
title claim, pledge, custody dispute or competing gift condition was
identified in the materials examined for this accession.

This assessment has a defined scope. Zero token-specific approval does not
exclude an approval-for-all operator. Public wallet records do not expose
every private agreement, legal claim or security incident. No universal
certificate of freedom from encumbrance is asserted. These limits remain
part of ordinary stewardship and do not create a request for more donor
rights information. Any later adverse claim or custody discrepancy requires
a dated registrar assessment.

Contract administration is described in the
[technical review](technical-and-condition-review.md). Metadata authority
remains distinct from Museum token custody and photographic copyright.

## Exact-address sanctions screen

On 13 September 2026, the Museum used the official
[OFAC Sanctions List Search](https://sanctionssearch.ofac.treas.gov/).
The digital-currency-address field was searched with all other filters at
All or blank and minimum name score 100. A known listed address first
returned CHATEX, CYBER2, SDN. The donor, Museum custody address, token
contract, implementation, owner, four admins and registered extension then
each returned zero results: ten exact-address searches in total.

The [screening record](../../../../evidence/salgado-amazonia-diligence/ofac-address-screening.json)
retains the individual observation times, exact addresses, rendered results,
positive control and displayed list-update dates. It records structured
observations of the official interface, without claiming retained HTTP
response bytes.

The finding is limited to those addresses at that time. It does not identify
the civil person behind a wallet, screen names, trace transaction exposure,
apply the 50 Percent Rule, search other authorities' lists or establish a
general legal clearance.
