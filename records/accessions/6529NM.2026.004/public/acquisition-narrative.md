# A gift of six Amazônia photographs

Punk6529 offered six photographs by Sebastião Salgado as *Amazônia: Forest,
Water, and Community*. The group joins forest, mountain and aerial views to
two photographs made in Indigenous communities. Its purpose is to allow
different parts of the project to be read together: the large distances of
the aerial photographs, the density of the forest vegetation, and the closer
encounters of portrait and community scene.

The proposal identified exactly tokens 226, 1117, 2059, 2216, 2768 and 4697
in Ethereum contract `0xf1f036cebb4b64f138e4f57754edbceb64fb80fc`. They were
offered as one decision unit, at no cost to the Museum. The Museum title and
selection belong to this acquisition; they are not an artist-authored edition
sequence.[^1]

The Museum Wave adopted the proposal. An authenticated readback completed on
13 September 2026 at 21:32:17 UTC reports `WINNER` and an API signed status
for serial 1344468. The six tokens were delivered earlier that evening in a
single successful transaction, at 20:15:35 UTC. Their continued ownership by
the Museum was verified at a common finalized block.[^2]

The donor also directly confirmed that the gift is unrestricted and instructed
ordinary Museum display and care. This confirmation supplies the gift terms
for the title record. The Museum accepts the six objects for accession
processing with no additional donor rights questionnaire. Copyright credit
remains with the applicable rights holders, separately from the donor credit.

The six catalogue entries develop the reason for the grouping through the
photographs themselves. The Jaú image separates individual trunks and fronds
within dense growth; the aerial river view makes a channel legible over a large
area. Mountains and weather introduce other ways of describing distance.
The portrait and community scene return attention to people whose histories
extend beyond the environmental argument of the group. These differences
give the acquisition a more particular shape than a survey of the project's
categories alone.

**Registration status:** Gift accepted for accession processing under the
donor's and Museum maintainer's instruction. Lot `6529NM.2026.004` and six
object identifiers are allocated for that work. The completed draft records
require independent review and execution of the accession certificate before
permanent Collection admission is recorded.
