# A gift of six Amazônia photographs


Punk6529 donated six photographs by [Sebastião Salgado](/museum/network/artists/sebastiao-salgado) to the 6529 Network Museum on 13 September 2026. The Museum accessioned them as **6529NM.2026.004**, objects **.01–.06**, under the title *Amazônia: Forest, Water, and Community*.[^1]

The gift comprises four landscapes and two photographs made in the Indigenous communities of Maronal and Nova Esperança.

The Museum Wave adopted the proposal for the six-work gift. Delivery, gift acceptance and accession are documented in the [source chronology](source-and-chronology.md) and [accession certificate](../accession-certificate.json).[^2]

The gift is unrestricted. The credit is **Gift of punk6529. Photograph © Sebastião Salgado.**[^3]
