# Amazônia: Forest, Water, and Community

Six photographs by Sebastião Salgado. Gift of punk6529. Accession
`6529NM.2026.004`, objects .01–.06.

Made between 2016 and 2019, the photographs bring together forest, mountains,
water and weather, a portrait at Maronal and a gathering at Nova Esperança.
They come from Salgado's 2022 *Amazônia* digital edition.
