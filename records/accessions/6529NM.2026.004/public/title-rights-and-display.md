## Museum use determination

The Museum takes the position that it may display and publish these works in
the ordinary course of its collection, exhibition, catalogue and educational
work. It may retain the source files for care and documentation and prepare
faithful technical copies for those Museum uses. This determination follows
the donor's express instruction and the Museum's established treatment of
acquired photographic works.

Copyright remains with the applicable rights holders. The unrestricted gift
does not assign the photographer's copyright or place the images under CC0.
The Museum grants no general licence for third-party reproduction, commercial
exploitation, sublicensing or model training. Source credits remain attached
to the works; the donor credit identifies the gift separately.

For web display, technical copies will preserve the complete framing and
orientation. The documented process will convert the embedded Adobe RGB
profile to sRGB, retain the source files unchanged, and record each copy's
dimensions, transform and checksum. Display copies will carry the photographer,
source caption, date, gift credit and copyright notice. Ordinary display does
not depend on obtaining another answer from the donor or soliciting a new
permission letter by default.
