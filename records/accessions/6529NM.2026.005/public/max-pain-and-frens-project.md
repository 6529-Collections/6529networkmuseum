# *MAX PAIN AND FRENS*

*MAX PAIN AND FRENS* is a 2022 release by XCOPY on Nifty Gateway. The artist's site divides the release into four parts: the open edition *MAX PAIN* and three variant editions.[^1] The release let buyers in a ten-minute window set the size of its main edition. The artist's later *DAMAGE CONTROL* turned that size into a falling count, and a third-party contract has continued to reduce it. Edition 4188, the Museum's, came through each change unburned.

## The sale

The open edition went on sale on 24 March 2022 from 22:30 to 22:40 UTC at US$3,108, a price Nifty Gateway had said would be about 1 ETH at drop time. Each checkout could take one edition, the edition had no maximum size, and payment was by card or prepaid ETH balance.[^2] When the window closed the platform recorded 7,394 editions. One of them had been reserved by the artist and the Nifty Gateway team for a giveaway announced on 21 March, and the platform's data do not separate it from the editions sold.[^3] Nifty Gateway then minted all 7,394 tokens into its custody wallet in 21 transactions between 23:38 and 23:43 UTC.[^4]

## The variant editions

The open edition shares the release with three variant editions on a separate contract: *WASTER* in six works, *DAMAGER LUXE* in twelve, and *GOURMET SPICY* in twenty-four. The artist's site describes each variant edition as unique works with derivative traits.[^5] A preview published on the day of the drop said the first two would be sold by ranked auction and the third by a US$1 drawing for holders of Nifty Gateway tokens.[^6] The release thus set 7,394 identical tokens of one image beside 42 unique variant works, which the Museum has not examined.

The preview also announced redemptions built on the artist's earlier *Afterburn*: holders could burn an *Afterburn* token to enter a drawing for *Afterburn Green*, or burn two to buy an open edition of *DAMAGER*, *GUZZLER RED*, or *REACHBACK* for US$1.[^6] The artist's series page does not list these redemptions, and no *MAX PAIN* token was burned before December 2023.[^7]

## Stated inspiration

In an interview published the day before the drop, XCOPY said it was inspired by "the chaos, adrenaline and risk in this space" and that the artist had tried to put some of that into the release.[^8] Press reports the next day led with the ten minutes and with the total, which at the listed price comes to just under US$23 million.[^9]

## *DAMAGE CONTROL*

*DAMAGE CONTROL* was paid for in *MAX PAIN* tokens. The series comprises ten works issued between 11 December 2023 and 8 November 2024 on an ERC-1155 contract built with Manifold and deployed by the address named xcopy.eth; four days after *OBLIVION*, the first of them, XCOPY announced that the burn-redeems of 2024 would mint there.[^10] Each redemption burned *MAX PAIN* tokens and took a payment in ETH. *OBLIVION*, *SIDEWAYZ*, *CRAWLER*, *XOMBO*, and *REIGN* each took one token and 0.1 ETH; *BANG_BANG* took one token and 0.2 ETH; *CHURN* and *HEAVY* took two tokens and 0.1 ETH; *SH_MASH_MA* and *BOT_ROT* took one *MAX PAIN*, one *DAMAGE CONTROL* token, and 0.1 ETH.[^11]

The redemption windows varied in length. *OBLIVION*'s 500 editions were redeemed in under half an hour on 11 December 2023. *BANG_BANG* and *SIDEWAYZ* opened in 24-hour windows on 10 January 2024, and all 230 of *BANG_BANG*'s burn-redemptions came within 72 seconds of its opening. The other seven stayed open until 23:59 UTC on 31 December 2024. In all, collectors made 2,283 redemptions and burned 2,545 *MAX PAIN* tokens.[^11]

On 21 March 2024 XCOPY posted the *MAX PAIN* image as a rare 2022 edition of 5,965, the chain count at that hour. On 28 July 2024 the artist posted the edition counts of the seven *DAMAGE CONTROL* works then released and set 31 December as the close.[^12]

## After the redemptions

A third-party contract has continued the burns. MaxPainFeeHandler, whose verified source credits TokenWorks, buys *MAX PAIN* tokens from a TokenWorks strategy contract that purchases them on the market with trading fees and relists them; it then burns each token and pays the caller a small reward.[^13] It burned edition 5741 on 1 February 2026 after paying 0.6675 ETH, and edition 3265 on 1 September 2026 after paying 0.6061 ETH. At block 26,218,159 on 18 October 2026, 4,847 tokens existed; XCOPY's work page shows 4,849, the count when *DAMAGE CONTROL* closed.[^14]

## The Museum's edition

Edition 4188 was minted at 23:39:21 UTC on 24 March 2022 and spent the whole *DAMAGE CONTROL* period in one private wallet; it entered the Museum's wallet on 2 September 2026.[^15] Its title still reads /7394, while the artist's site counts 4,849 and the chain 4,847. The burn contract acts only on tokens the strategy contract holds for sale, so edition 4188 is beyond it while the Museum does not offer it for sale.[^13]

## An open upper bound

The issuing contract has not closed the *MAX PAIN* type to further minting: read on 18 October 2026, it did not mark the type as final.[^16] No token beyond the 7,394 issued identifiers has been observed, and whether any account can still mint now that Nifty Gateway has closed is unknown. The edition's upper bound of 7,394 therefore rests on practice rather than on code.
