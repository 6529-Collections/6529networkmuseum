# MAX PAIN #4188/7394

XCOPY's *MAX PAIN* #4188/7394 is a square black field almost filled by a rounded teal head. Scraped strokes and a fine teal-and-navy dither build the head, and ragged black bars cross its dome. A broad pink band runs across the face. On it sit two black eyes, each crowned by a curling mint flame and holding a small pink square with a black pupil. Below, loose teal blocks above a heavy teal bar form a grille-like mouth. A mint telephone handset marks the left side of the head; at upper right, a small pink skull in a pale teal ring sits at the tip of one of the thin black-and-white checkered streaks that fall through the image. The GIF repeats every 0.4 seconds: the band flips between hot pink and magenta, and the flames are redrawn on every frame.[^1] This token is edition 4188 of the 7,394 that Nifty Gateway issued on 24 March 2022, and one of 4,847 that existed on 18 October 2026.[^2]

**Medium:** Animated GIF, 4,000 × 4,000 pixels, eight frames of 50 milliseconds looping without end; Ethereum ERC-721 token whose metadata names the image by IPFS hash; Nifty Gateway open edition  
**Edition:** Open edition; 7,394 tokens issued, 4,847 existing on 18 October 2026; token `171000104188`, titled #4188/7394  
**Accession:** `6529NM.2026.005`  
**Object:** `6529NM.2026.005.01`  
**CAIP-19:** `eip155:1/erc721:0xd1169e5349d1cb9941f3dcba135c8a4b9eacfdde/171000104188`  
**Token IPFS image hash:** `QmTcTzTPgCyvF933oeFvB4GkPgaXtF6dKuQgvg32qqLYGF`

Two cycles run through the eight frames. Each frame is held for 50 milliseconds, and successive frames alternate between two color states. In one state the band is a coarse dither of hot pink and red-pink; in the other it is magenta and hot pink. The pink accents on the dome, the skull, and the pupil squares shift with the band, and the checkered streaks trade one black-and-white pattern for another. A second cycle runs inside the eyes, where each of the eight frames carries its own drawing of the flames and a slightly different pupil. Everything else that changes returns every tenth of a second, while the flames never repeat within a loop.[^3]

About 81 percent of the image never changes. The black ground, the teal head and its texture, the strokes on the dome, the three small slots above the eyes, the teal column of small black squares between them, the grille, the handset, and the ring around the skull hold still in every frame.[^3] Because the head itself never moves, the flicker reads as a disturbance of its surface: a fixed face across which color and pattern keep switching.

The palette is small and hard-edged. All 44 colors in the file fall on the 216-color web-safe grid, and its soft-looking passages are two-color dithers: hot pink against red-pink in the band, teal against navy in the head. Black covers about two-thirds of the image across the loop; teal with its navy and dark-teal dither takes about a sixth, and the pinks about a ninth.[^4]

The GIF is the work's constitutive file. The token's metadata names it by IPFS hash and also gives a Nifty Gateway CDN address. On 18 October 2026 the Museum downloaded the file from both locations, found the bytes identical, and recomputed the IPFS identifier from them; it matches the hash in the metadata and the contract's `tokenIPFSHash` record for this token.[^5] The Museum's poster is the first of the eight frames, which records one of the two color states and one of the eight flame drawings.

*MAX PAIN* is the open edition of *MAX PAIN AND FRENS*, released on Nifty Gateway beside three variant editions of unique works; see [*MAX PAIN AND FRENS*](max-pain-and-frens-project.md). Nifty Gateway minted the 7,394 tokens on 24 March 2022, in 21 transactions between 23:38 and 23:43 UTC. Collectors burned 2,545 of them to redeem XCOPY's *DAMAGE CONTROL* works between 11 December 2023 and 31 December 2024, and a third-party contract burned two more in February and September 2026. At block 26,218,159 on 18 October 2026, 4,847 existed.[^6] XCOPY's work page shows 4,849, the count when *DAMAGE CONTROL* closed; the token's title still counts 7,394.[^7]
