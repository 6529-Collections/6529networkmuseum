# *MAX PAIN* #4188/7394: A Community-Funded Acquisition

A rounded teal head fills a black square. A broad hot-pink band clamps across the middle of the face, and on it sit two large black eyes, each crowned by a curling mint-green flame. Below, rows of teal blocks form a grille-like mouth, and thin black-and-white checkered streaks run down through the face. The animation is eight frames long and lasts four tenths of a second. On every frame the band switches between hot pink and magenta, the streaks flicker, and the flames are redrawn.[^looking]

This is XCOPY's *MAX PAIN*, edition 4188 of 7,394, from a ten-minute open edition sold on Nifty Gateway on 24 March 2022.[^drop] On 2 September 2026, with money raised in twenty-five equal shares from members of the 6529 network, it was bought and transferred to the 6529 Network Museum's wallet.

*MAX PAIN* is usually remembered as market history: a mass drop, and later the raw material for XCOPY's burns. That history is the work's subject. XCOPY sold one image to thousands of collectors and later asked them to spend it, and a third of the edition has since been destroyed. Edition 4188 came through intact, and in 2026 twenty-five people paid for it so that a museum could keep it.

## Looking

The distortion is drawn into the face. The teal is laid down in scraped strokes and coarse dither, and pink slashes cut across the dome; there is no smooth face beneath for the streaks to have corrupted. About four-fifths of the image never changes: the teal head, the grille, a telephone handset at the left, the ring around a small skull at the upper right. Nearly all the flicker sits in the band, the streaks, and the burning eyes, where a viewer looks first, while smaller pink accents, the skull among them, switch color in step. The loop has no climax. It repeats two and a half times a second, and the face holds its shape through the interference without settling into a portrait.

The title echoes a term from options trading, where "max pain" is the strike price at which the greatest value of open contracts would expire worthless, the outcome that costs option holders the most.[^maxpain] XCOPY described the drop in broader terms: in a question-and-answer piece Nifty Gateway published the day before the sale, the artist said it was inspired by "the chaos, adrenaline and risk in this space."[^qa] Read beside the trading term, the face looks held at the point of greatest loss, the band clamped across its eyes and the loop running on without reaching a still frame.

## The edition

The sale ran from 22:30 to 22:40 UTC at US$3,108, about 1 ETH at the time, and Nifty Gateway issued 7,394 editions.[^drop] About an hour after the sale closed, Nifty Gateway minted them to its own custody address, and buyers withdrew them to their own wallets from there; edition 4188 left on 25 March.[^mint] Owning one placed a collector among thousands who held the same face. In October 2026 the editions sat in 3,225 addresses.[^count]

Four months after the sale, on 1 August 2022, XCOPY placed all existing XCOPY works, *MAX PAIN* among them, under a CC0 dedication.[^cc0] The dedication gave up control over copies, and XCOPY's pseudonymity gives up biography: there is no known life story to explain the image's mood.

An edition that size turned the image into a denomination, a unit thousands of XCOPY's collectors held in common, and XCOPY later priced new work in it. From December 2023 to November 2024 the artist released *DAMAGE CONTROL*, ten works each redeemed by burning one or two *MAX PAIN* editions and paying ETH, usually 0.1.[^dc] By the close of redemption on 31 December 2024, collectors had burned 2,545 editions to redeem 2,283 tokens of the ten new works, in 2,139 transactions from 1,215 addresses. *DAMAGE CONTROL* contracted what *MAX PAIN* had expanded, and the contraction depended on the expansion: the denomination had to circulate before collectors could choose to destroy it. In 2026 a third-party program that buys editions on the market began burning some of them, and two more were destroyed. On 18 October 2026, 4,847 remained.[^count]

Edition 4188 sat out that history. One wallet held it from 26 March 2022 to 1 September 2026, through all ten redemptions, and it was never burned.[^provenance]

## The acquisition

The Museum Wave is the network's voting forum for the Museum. On 24 August 2026 Shelby posted a proposal there to acquire one original *MAX PAIN*. Every member who voted for it committed to an equal share of the final cost, and contributors would receive no individual or fractional ownership interest. Twenty-five members voted in favor. The proposal crossed its threshold of 69,000,000 TDH (Total Days Held, the network's time-weighted measure of collecting) on 26 August and was declared a winner on 27 August.[^wave]

The organizer set each share at 0.021 ETH.[^funding] The vote approved one edition without naming it; the organizer chose edition 4188 at purchase. On 2 September the acquisition wallet, which by then had received 0.4646 ETH, bought it for 0.459 ETH, and twenty-eight minutes later it transferred the token to the Museum's wallet.[^chain] A further 0.063 ETH, three shares' worth, arrived after the purchase, 0.5276 ETH in all, and on 14 September the organizer sent the remaining 0.0685 ETH to the Museum's wallet.[^residual]

Twenty-five people paid for an XCOPY none of them would own, so that the Museum could hold one. With receipt the purchase was complete, and the keeping passed to the Museum: the token, its file, and the record of how it was paid for.

On 29 August 2026 the wallet that later sold edition 4188 to the acquisition wallet passed edition 3265 of *MAX PAIN* to the burning program's strategy contract. On 1 September, the day before the purchase, the program's burn contract bought edition 3265 and burned it.[^3265] Edition 3265 now exists only as a burn recorded on chain. Edition 4188, the same flickering face, is in the Museum's wallet, and its image remains free for anyone to copy.

## Museum note

The Museum received edition 4188 in its wallet, `networkmuseum.6529.eth`, on 2 September 2026 and accessioned it on 18 October 2026 as object `6529NM.2026.005.01`, recording the acquisition as a community-funded purchase. The organizer announced it as the first XCOPY donated to the Museum.[^announce] No earlier lot in the Museum's accession register, `6529NM.2026.001` to `6529NM.2026.003`, includes a work by XCOPY.[^register] On 14 September the wallet also received 0.0685 ETH left over from the contributions, with the organizer's request that it fund future XCOPY purchases. The Museum has received the funds but has not accepted them, and has asked the organizer how they should be held. The token's metadata points its image to a Nifty Gateway server, and Nifty Gateway closed in April 2026. The Museum keeps the original file, which matches the IPFS hash recorded in the token contract. Shown large, the animation exceeds the WCAG general-flash threshold, so the Museum shows a still image first and plays the animation only on request, at a limited size. The Museum credits all twenty-five contributors by name, the organizer among them; the proposal asked the same share of each.
