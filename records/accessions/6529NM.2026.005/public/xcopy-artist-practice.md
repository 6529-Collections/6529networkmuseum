# XCOPY: The Loop and Its Terms

XCOPY makes short looping animations and releases them as tokens, chiefly on Ethereum, under a pseudonym. In a 2022 interview published by Nifty Gateway, XCOPY said that the artist's parents and a handful of friends knew who was behind the name.[^1] The practice therefore reaches the public through works, titles, release terms, and the artist's site and posts. Read together, those sources show the looping GIF persisting as the unit of release in every case the Museum has examined, while the terms of its circulation (edition size, destruction, and copyright) have become material the artist works with.

## The feed

The earliest surviving post on XCOPY's Tumblr is a photo post of 18 August 2010, tagged as noise collage.[^2] In the 2022 interview the artist credited Tumblr with making art-making feel like a game: the platform's limits shaped the GIFs, and motion was a way to stand out from everything else in the feed.[^1] In *MAX PAIN*, the work the Museum holds, the motion is a 0.4-second loop of eight frames whose colors switch on every frame, fast enough to register at a glance in a scrolling page.[^3]

## From Tumblr to tokens

According to Sotheby's, XCOPY encountered crypto in 2017 and began making tokenized work in 2018.[^4] The artist has said that the first twelve tokens minted on SuperRare were conceived as a set.[^1] The interviewer also relayed a collector's theory of a story running through titles of 2018, among them *Death Dip*, *Coin for the Ferryman*, and *Post Debt*; XCOPY answered that the work is kept open to interpretation.[^1] The titles set the vocabulary of trading beside the iconography of death. The pairing returns in *MAX PAIN*, whose title is a trading phrase and whose image carries a small pink skull at upper right.[^3]

## The GIF in later releases

In the three releases from 2022 to 2024 examined here, the unit is still a GIF. *MAX PAIN* (2022) is a 4,000-pixel GIF of eight frames.[^3] *EXIT STRATEGY*, the artist's December 2022 card for The Memes by 6529, is served as a GIF.[^5] The ten *DAMAGE CONTROL* works of 2023 and 2024 are recorded in their token metadata as 4,096-pixel GIFs.[^6] *MAX PAIN* uses 44 of the 256 colors a GIF allows, all from the web-safe grid, and lets two-color dithers stand in for gradients.[^3] The Museum has not examined the file formats of the later *BUBBLE(S)* and *FIELDS* releases.

## Editions as material

*MAX PAIN* was an open edition of 7,394 issued beside 42 unique variants, in a release Nifty Gateway billed as XCOPY's return after a year away from the platform.[^7] From December 2023 to the end of 2024, collectors redeemed *DAMAGE CONTROL* works by burning *MAX PAIN* tokens, and in February 2024 the artist wrote that all new editions would carry a damage-control requirement.[^8] In December 2025 the artist's site reported two million *BUBBLE(S)* minted; in August 2026 it introduced *FIELDS*, a collection for unique on-chain works.[^9] Edition sizes have run from a single token to two million, and some editions were issued in exchange for burning another XCOPY.

## Permissionless Art

The artist's Creative Commons page is headed "Permissionless Art" and states that XCOPY works are dedicated under CC0, adding that derivative and collaborative works may carry other rights.[^10] By March 2022 the artist had placed *Right Click and Save-as Guy* in the public domain, explaining that once an image is online it makes little sense to dictate what people do with it, and that platforms should not build systems to stamp out lookalikes.[^1] In a thread on 1 August 2022, the artist released one work into the public domain on the anniversary of its mint and then applied CC0 to all existing work.[^11] The site's legal page adds that independent uses imply no endorsement unless stated, and the site marked the dedication's third and fourth anniversaries with notes on its remix gallery.[^12] The dedication applied to every existing work the reasoning XCOPY had given in March: an image online travels regardless of what its maker permits.

## Recent work

Releases announced on the artist's site in 2025 and 2026 include *FRESH HELL* works such as *EMPIRE THINGS* (March 2025), *LAWS_INNIT* (September 2025), and *KILL YOUR CUSTOMER* (September 2026); *COIN LAUNDRY* (December 2025); *MISSION CREEP*, a collaboration with Lewis Osborne (June 2026); and *SERFIE*, the first *FIELDS* release (August 2026).[^9] The titles keep the early work's vocabulary of money and its machinery: laundering, customers, serfs, empire.

## Reception

The reception recorded here comes from the crypto press, an auction house, and an art fair. *Decrypt* and *Crypto Briefing* reported the *MAX PAIN* sale the day after it closed, leading with its speed and its total.[^13] Three days after the CC0 thread, *Decrypt* placed the dedication within a wider account of artists giving up commercial rights to their work.[^14] In January 2024 Sotheby's offered *Blood Bubble* (2020), an edition of 25, in its online sale *GRAILS: Starry Night*. The lot essay described XCOPY as a pioneer of the crypto art movement and noted that *Right Click and Save-as Guy* had been shown on digital displays and murals, including in Times Square and Melbourne.[^4] The artist's site reports that Nguyen Wahed presented *COIN LAUNDRY* at Art Basel Miami Beach in December 2025.[^9]

## The Museum's work

The 6529 Network Museum holds *MAX PAIN* #4188/7394 together with the 4,000-pixel GIF its token names by IPFS hash. That file allows the practice's unit to be studied frame by frame: its eight 50-millisecond frames, its 44 colors, and the two cycles that drive its flicker are recorded in the Museum's condition and technical record and read in the [object entry](6529NM.2026.005.01.md).[^3]
