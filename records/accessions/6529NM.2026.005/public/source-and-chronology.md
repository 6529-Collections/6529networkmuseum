## Status of the residual funds

On 14 September 2026 the Museum wallet received 0.068496911687069084 ETH from the organizer's acquisition wallet, with the organizer's request that the funds pay for future XCOPY purchases. The Museum has received the funds but has not accepted them, and has asked the organizer how they should be held. The residual is outside the accession of the token. About 0.0000284 ETH remains in the acquisition wallet after gas.[^17]
