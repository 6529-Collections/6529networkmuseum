# Themes and Variations #210

Vera Molnár and Martin Grasser’s *Themes and Variations* #210 is a square field of cobalt and warm white. A rough-edged blue mass presses into the upper-right corner; below it, five white T forms descend through a second blue field. At right, five narrow bars hang from a single horizontal line. A small F sits near the left edge, while a stippled blue panel compresses the lower-right corner. The letters remain readable as letters, then become blocks, bars, and texture. This is one unique output in a fixed edition of 500. The PNG records a dated rendering; the live Art Blocks generator supplies the work’s current browser display.
